# AI-Based Cardiac Anomaly Detection using IoT

This project presents an advanced AI-based healthcare system designed for cardiac anomaly detection, continuous post-health monitoring, and emergency tele-consultation using Internet of Things (IoT) technology. The system aims to provide a smart, efficient, and reliable solution for real-time heart health monitoring, enabling early detection of potential cardiac abnormalities and improving the overall quality of patient care.

The system utilizes IoT-enabled sensors to continuously collect real-time physiological data such as heart rate from the user. The collected data is processed through a microcontroller and securely transmitted to a cloud-based platform using wireless communication technologies such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. This enables seamless remote monitoring and centralized storage of patient health records.

Artificial Intelligence plays a key role in the system by analyzing the collected physiological data to identify abnormal heart patterns and detect possible cardiac anomalies. The AI-based analysis helps in providing early warnings, allowing timely medical intervention and reducing the risks associated with delayed diagnosis.

A significant feature of this project is its ability to support continuous post-health monitoring. Patients can regularly monitor their cardiac health even after diagnosis or treatment, enabling doctors and healthcare professionals to track recovery progress and provide better long-term healthcare management.

In emergency situations, whenever abnormal readings or critical cardiac conditions are detected, the system generates instant alerts and facilitates emergency tele-consultation by connecting patients with doctors or nearby healthcare providers. This ensures quick medical assistance and enhances patient safety during critical situations.

The project integrates Embedded Systems, IoT communication, Cloud Computing, and Artificial Intelligence to build a comprehensive smart healthcare solution. It demonstrates how modern technologies can work together to improve healthcare accessibility, enable remote patient monitoring, support preventive diagnosis, and provide efficient healthcare services for individuals irrespective of their location.

Overall, this project highlights the potential of AI and IoT in transforming traditional healthcare systems into intelligent, connected, and proactive healthcare solutions capable of delivering accurate monitoring, early anomaly detection, and timely medical assistance.
